**Type in dots. Use VerseBoard.**

VerseBoard is an open source Android keyboard by **Araf Studio**, built around the Nothing dot-matrix look. Bengali layouts (Avro, Jatiyo, Probhat), swipe typing, smart predictions, a personal dictionary, clipboard history and a colorful matte key theme, all in one clean dark interface.

Available on **Android**, in two builds: one for modern devices and one for old devices.

---

## Download

Get the APK from the [**Releases page**](https://github.com/arafstudio/verseboard/releases/latest).

| APK | Best for | Android |
|---|---|---|
| `VerseBoard-v1.0.0-android7plus.apk` | Modern and good devices (recommended) | **7.0 and above** |
| `VerseBoard-v1.0.0-universal.apk` | Old and low-end devices | **5.0 – 7.1** |

> Not sure? Phone from 2017 or later: take **android7plus**. Older or slow phone: take **universal**.

### Install

1. Download the APK for your device.
2. Open it and allow **Install from unknown sources** if asked.
3. Open **VerseBoard**, enable it in *Settings → System → Keyboard*, and select it as your keyboard.

## Features

- **Bengali layouts**: Avro (phonetic), Jatiyo (national standard), Probhat
- **Nothing Dot font** keys, with a normal system font option
- **Swipe typing** (alpha)
- **Matte key colors**: yellow, blue, red, white and dark spacebar styles
- **Text prediction**: correction, next-word and personalized suggestions (stored only on your device)
- **Personal dictionary** and blacklisted suggestions
- **Emoji suggestions** and Apple-style emoji (loaded online once)
- **Clipboard history** and long-press shortcuts (C, X, V)
- **Number row**, suggestion row, one-hand mode, floating and split keyboard
- **Resize keyboard**: height and offset
- **Tools**: translator, writing tools, scan text, voice typing, undo
- **Extra languages**: Spanish, French, German, Turkish, Portuguese, Italian, Hindi, Arabic
